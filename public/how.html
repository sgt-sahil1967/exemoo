How it works

1

Pick a game

Open Exemoo on your computer or TV and choose any game from the list.

2

Scan the QR code

Point your phone camera at the QR code on screen. Your phone turns into a controller — no app needed.

3

Move to play

Swing, slice, lean and pose — your phone's motion sensors control the game in real time.

No phone? No problem.

Every game also works with keyboard & mouse. Use ←→↑↓ to move, Enter to select, or just click.

Keep your phone and computer on the same Wi-Fi
Tap "Allow" when your phone asks for motion access
Hold your phone firmly — and give yourself room!
